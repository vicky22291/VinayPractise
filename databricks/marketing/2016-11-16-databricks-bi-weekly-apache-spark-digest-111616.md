## Spark Summit Talks and Apache Spark Roundup

- Databricks and partners set a new world record for [CloudSort 2016 Benchmark using Apache Spark](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/11/14/setting-new-world-record-apache-spark.html), wrote Reynold Xin, chief architect.
- Databricks Chief Technologist Matei Zaharia delivered a keynote, “[Simplifying Big Data Applications with Apache Spark 2.0](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit),” at Spark Summit 2016 EU in Brussels, followed by a demo of continuous application by Databricks software engineer Greg Owen.
- Databricks CEO Ali Ghodsi shared his vision of “[Democratizing AI with Apache Spark](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit)” in his keynote at Spark Summit 2016 EU in Brussels.
- Executive Chairman of Databricks Ion Stoica announced “[The Next AmpLAB: Real-time, Intelligent, and Secure Computing](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit),” in his keynote at Spark Summit 2016 EU in Brussels.
- Sameer Agarwal, software engineer at Databricks, presented “[Apache Spark’s Performance: Project Tungsten and Beyond](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit),” at Spark Summit 2016 EU in Brussels.
- Herman Van Hovell, software engineer at Databricks, gave a “[Deep Dive into the Catalyst Optimizer](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit)” talk and a [hands-on lab](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit) at Spark Summit 2016 EU in Brussels.
- Echoing Ali Ghodsi’s keynote above, Tim Hunter, Databricks software engineer, showed how to use Apache Spark with TensorFlow: “[TensorFrames: Deep Learning with TensorFlow on Apache Spark,](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit)” at Spark Summit 2016 EU in Brussels.
- Databricks Solution Architect Mikos Christine shared challenges and pitfalls you can avoid with Spark Streaming in his talk “[Paddling up the Stream](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit),” at Spark Summit 2016 EU in Brussels.
- Facebook’s Big Compute Team software engineer Sital Kedia described how Apache Spark scales in production in his talk: “[Apache Spark at Scale: A 60 TB+ Production Use Case](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit)” at Spark Summit 2016 EU in Brussels.
- Morning Paper blogger Adrian Colyer commented on Michael Armbrust et. al. article “[Scaling Spark in the Real World: Performance and Usability.](https://cs.stanford.edu/~matei/papers/2015/vldb_spark.pdf)”
- Matei Zaharia, Reynold Xin et.al. contributed to Communications of ACM: “[Apache Spark: A Unified Engine for Big Data Processing.](https://cacm.acm.org/magazines/2016/11/209116-apache-spark/fulltext)”
- Tim Hunter, Databricks software engineer, participated on the panel "[Modern Software Architectures and Data Pipelines](https://scalaebythebay2016.sched.com/event/8auf?iframe=no)" at [Scala by the Bay](https://scalaebythebay2016.sched.com/).

## Releases

- [GraphFrames 0.3.0](https://spark-packages.org/package/graphframes/graphframes) released as a spark package. Find out more from graphframes.github.io.
- [Apache Spark 1.6.3 Released](https://spark.apache.org/news/spark-1-6-3-released.html). Try it on [Databricks Community Edition](https://www.databricks.com/try-databricks).
- [Apache Spark 2.0.2 Released](https://spark.apache.org/news/spark-2-0-2-released.html). Kafka 0.10 support and runtime metrics are the two notable features in Structured Streaming in this release. Try it on [Databricks Community Edition](https://www.databricks.com/try-databricks).
- Databricks released [spark-redshift v3.0.0-preview1](https://github.com/databricks/spark-redshift/tree/v3.0.0-preview1) spark package with usability improvements. Learn more about its improvements at [Redshift Data Source for Apache Spark](https://github.com/databricks/spark-redshift/releases/tag/v3.0.0-preview1).
