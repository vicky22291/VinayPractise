Here’s our recap of what’s transpired with Apache Spark since our [previous digest](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/08/31/databricks-bi-weekly-digest-83116.html).

- Databricks [Apache Spark Survey 2016 Report](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/09/27/spark-survey-2016-released.html) published and now available.
- Databricks’ Chief Architect Reynold Xin joined Intel’s Michael Greene in a keynote at the JavaOne Conference: [Apache Spark is the Pokemon Go of Big Data](https://jaxenter.com/javaone-2016-java-keynote-live-129183.html).
- Apache Spark and Databricks won [Datanami Readers’ and Editors’ Choice Awards for Machine Learning, Real-time Analytics, Best Startup and More..](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/09/19/apache-spark-earns-datanami-awards-for-machine-learning-real-time-analytics-and-more.html)
- Databricks’ Chief Technologist and Co-founder Matei Zaharia presented “[Unifying big data workloads in Apache Spark](https://atscaleconference.com/videos/unifying-big-data-workloads-in-apache-spark/)” at [@Scale Conference](https://atscaleconference.com/events/main-event/).
- Apache Spark Community Voice from CERN: Luca Canali of CERN shared [Apache Spark 2.0 Performance Improvements Investigated With Flame Graphs](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/10/03/voice-from-cern-apache-spark-2-0-performance-improvements-investigated-with-flame-graphs.html) in his blog.
- Databricks’ Jules S. Damji and Sameer Farooqui published [7 Steps to Mastering Apache Spark 2.0](https://www.kdnuggets.com/2016/09/7-steps-mastering-apache-spark.html) in KDnuggets.
- Databricks released integration utilities for using [Apache Spark with Apache Avro data](https://spark-packages.org/package/databricks/spark-avro) as a Spark package.
- Databricks’ Ram Sriharsha and Xiangrui Meng conducted [Ask Me Anything: State of Apache Spark](https://www.oreilly.com/conferences/) at Strata + Hadoop World NYC.
- Databricks’ Xiangrui Meng presented [Recent Development in SparkR for Advanced Analytics](https://www.oreilly.com/conferences/) at Strata + Hadoop World NYC
- Databricks’ Ram Sriharsha presented [The State of Apache Spark and What’s Next after Apache Spark 2.0](https://www.oreilly.com/conferences/) at Strata + Hadoop World NYC.
- [Apache Spark 2.0.1 Released.](https://spark.apache.org/releases/spark-release-2-0-1.html) Try it on [Databricks Community Edition](https://www.databricks.com/try-databricks).
