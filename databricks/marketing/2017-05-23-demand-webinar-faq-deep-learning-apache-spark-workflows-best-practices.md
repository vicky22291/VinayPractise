On May 4th, we hosted a live webinar — [Deep Learning and Apache Spark: Workflows and Best Practices](https://www.databricks.com/). Rather than comparing deep learning systems or specific optimizations, this webinar focused on issues that are common to deep learning frameworks when running on an Apache Spark cluster, including:

1. how to optimize cluster setup;
2. how to ideally configure the Spark cluster;
3. how to ingest data; and
4. how to monitor long-running jobs?
