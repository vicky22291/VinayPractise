## Webinar

- Joseph Bradley and I presented [Apache Spark MLlib 2.x: Migrating ML Workloads to DataFrames](https://www.databricks.com/), and posted the follow up [questions & answers blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/12/14/demand-webinar-faq-apache-spark-mllib-2-x-migrating-ml-workloads-dataframes.html).
