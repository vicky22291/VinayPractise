Continuing with our bi-weekly digest series, here’s our recap of what’s transpired over the last two weeks with Apache Spark since our [previous digest.](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/07/18/databricks-bi-weekly-digest-71816.html)

- Databricks announced [general availability of Apache Spark 2.0](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/07/26/introducing-apache-spark-2-0.html) on its just-in-time data platform. Reynold Xin elaborated on the merits of Spark 2.0, as easier, faster, and smarter.
- Databricks’ CTO and Co-founder Matei Zaharia envisaged the evolution of real-time streaming in Apache Spark 2.0 with [Continuous Applications](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/07/28/continuous-applications-evolving-streaming-in-apache-spark-2-0.html).
- Messrs Matei Zaharia, Tathagata Das, Michael Armbrust, and Reynold Xin elaborated on [Structured Streaming Model and its API](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/07/28/structured-streaming-in-apache-spark.html) in Apache Spark 2.0: How to write end-to-end continuous applications using the DataFrame and Dataset based streaming API.
- Jax Magazine interviewed Xiangrui Meng about [Apache MLlib - Making practical machine learning easier and scalable](https://jaxenter.com/apache-mllib-making-practical-machine-learning-easy-and-scalable-128037.html).
- Xiangrui Meng talked about Apache SparkR at the Walmart Meetup: Peruse the [tech-talk slides here](https://files.meetup.com/4439192/Recent%20Development%20in%20SparkR%20for%20Advanced%20Analytics.pdf?_cookie-check=bB0Z3qmiMAmAB6gp?_cookie-check=bB0Z3qmiMAmAB6gp).
- The [MongoDB Connector for Apache Spark](https://spark-packages.org/package/mongodb/mongo-spark) released as a Spark package, and [Sam Weaver’s guest blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/07/21/the-new-mongodb-connector-for-apache-spark-in-action-building-a-movie-recommendation-engine.html) demonstrated the connector using a Databricks notebook.
- Databricks released a new version of the [spark-redshift package](https://spark-packages.org/package/databricks/spark-redshift) that works with Spark 2.0.
- Kaarthik Sivashanmugam, tech lead for the Mobius Project at Microsoft, explained in a guest blog how [Mobius’ C# API extends and enables .NET developers](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/08/03/developing-apache-spark-applications-in-net-using-mobius.html) to write Apache Spark applications.
- KDD rated [Matrix Computations and Optimization in Apache Spark](http://www.kdd.org/kdd2016/subtopic/view/matrix-computations-and-optimization-in-apache-spark) as Best Runner up Paper in Applied Data Science Track for 2016.
- Daniel Pape opined on [Spark 2.0: Datasets and case classes](https://blog.codecentric.de/en/2016/07/spark-2-0-datasets-case-classes/) in a community blog.
- Databricks [announced the agenda](https://www.databricks.com/blog/2016/08/04/agenda-announced-for-sparksummit-europe-2016-in-brussels.html) for [Spark Summit Europe 2016](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit) in Brussels, Belgium. Register today!
